# Manifesto Data Sources

This document catalogs the reliable manifesto sources identified for the CIVICLENS TN Political Promise Tracker. The sources focus on major elections relevant to Tamil Nadu, primarily the 2021 Legislative Assembly Elections and the 2024 Lok Sabha Elections.

## Sourcing Guidelines

*   **Primary Priority:** Official political party websites, Election Commission sources, and recognized public institutional repositories.
*   **Data Integrity:** We do not invent manifesto content. Promises are extracted exclusively from full manifesto documents.
*   **Exclusions:** We do not infer promises from news headlines. Campaign speeches are not treated as manifesto promises unless explicitly designated as a separate, verified source type.
